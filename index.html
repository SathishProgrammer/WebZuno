08 — Questions

Before you ask.

The things local business owners usually want to know first.

How long does a website take?

Most Starter sites go live in 1–2 weeks and Growth sites in 2–4 weeks, once we have your content. Pro builds depend on scope — we agree a timeline before we start.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once the project is paid, the site, its files and your domain are yours. You’re never locked in to us.

What about domain and hosting?

We can register your domain and set up fast, secure hosting for you — or use the ones you already have. We explain every yearly cost up front.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. We can set up simple editing for things like prices, timings and photos, and show you how. Or just WhatsApp us the change and we’ll do it.

Will people find me on Google?

Every site ships with the SEO basics — fast pages, proper titles, Google Maps and Business Profile linking. Growth and Pro plans go further with local SEO.

What do I need to get started?

Just a short chat about your business. Logo, photos and text help, but if you don’t have them yet we’ll guide you through it.
